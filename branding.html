Mark rationale: the teal curve is the bowl — Hygeia's cup, historically the pharmacy/wellness symbol, distinct from the Rod of Asclepius (a staff, tied to clinical/medical authority). The gold line rising out of it is a reduced, single-stroke stand-in for the serpent — reads equally as a pulse or data signal, tying prevention/wellness to the data-intelligence positioning without a literal snake or a medical cross.

Why this fits the product: the mark deliberately avoids clinical iconography (no cross, no caduceus/staff, no stethoscope) — consistent with the platform's own constraint of not presenting itself as a clinical authority.

Scale test: the coil simplifies gracefully down to favicon size (top-right card) by dropping the second curve and rim details — full mark for anything ≥32px, reduced mark below that.
